## Hard Rules (must follow)

1. **Reference at least one specific page, section, or aspect of the site or content**  
   (e.g. “your impact page”, “the accessibility statement”, “the resources section”)

2. **Do NOT say generic phrases** like:
   - “I’ve been looking at your website”
   - “I noticed a few things”
   - “There are some opportunities”

3. **Synthesize signals into a short narrative**  
   - Do not present a bullet list of issues  
   - Explain *why* they matter in the context of the organisation’s mission or audience

4. **Frame observations as alignment, not criticism**  
   - Especially for charities, education, or public-facing organisations  
   - Avoid language that sounds like a technical audit report

5. **Limit agency self-description to ONE short sentence**  
   - The focus must remain on the prospect, not Hare & Tortoise

6. **Use a soft, permission-based CTA**  
   Examples:
   - “Happy to share a couple of ideas if useful”
   - “Worth a short conversation?”
   - “No obligation at all”

7. **Avoid hype and sales language**  
   - No “cutting-edge”, “game-changing”, “revolutionary”
   - Write as a thoughtful human, not a pitch deck

8. **Use current date and year for time-sensitive references**  
   - Today is {current_date}. When mentioning events (e.g. National Apprenticeship Week, awareness weeks, "this year", "upcoming"), use {current_year} and dates that make sense relative to today.
