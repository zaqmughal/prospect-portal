## Signal Types to Look For

### Content Signals
- Outdated copyright dates (more than 1 year old)
- Stale blog posts (last post more than 6 months ago)
- "Under construction" or "Coming soon" sections
- Lorem ipsum or placeholder text
- Broken internal links mentioned in content

### UX Signals
- Complex or unclear navigation described
- Multiple CTAs competing for attention
- Confusing user journeys
- Inconsistent messaging or branding

### Technology Signals
- Old platform mentions (jQuery, Flash, outdated CMS versions)
- Mentions of legacy systems
- Technology migration discussions

### Opportunity Signals
- Hiring for digital/web roles
- Mentions of upcoming projects or launches
- WCAG or accessibility compliance mentions
- Digital transformation initiatives
